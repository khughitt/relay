## Reader, lifecycle and runbook limits

Every open rebuilds from byte 0. Orphan outputs, repeated dispatch identities,
conflicting duplicates, wrong/unfinished proof, structured built-in output and
unknown file state fail closed. Every output classified other is discarded,
regardless of shape; only call id and an in-memory conflict fingerprint remain.
Fingerprints never reach evidence, logs, persistence or telemetry. Files over
64 MiB or lines over 1 MiB stay sticky unknown, including unrelated inline images.
The earlier local census was the external reviewer's measurement, not this agent's.

After a handed answer, a proven end without an answer remains `unconfirmed`:
this means a known end with no answer reaching the model, not possible eventual
receipt. Before handoff, the lifecycle expectation is asker-gone without a saved
answer, or undeliverable with one. These expectations are tested as units;
production inbox transitions have not been implemented or observed here.
Resolution revokes sending immediately; only the joint evidence can settle receipt.
A restart or disappearance from partial discovery is never a terminal proof.
